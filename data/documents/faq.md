# Frequently Asked Questions

## About Terra & Pine Outfitters

Terra & Pine Outfitters is an outdoor apparel and gear company founded in
2014 in Bend, Oregon. We design and sell hiking boots, backpacks, insulated
jackets, and camp cookware, and we ship to customers across the United
States, Canada, and the European Union. Our mission is to make durable,
repairable outdoor gear that reduces the need for frequent replacement.

## Creating an Account

You can create an account at checkout or from the account icon in the top
navigation bar. An account lets you save shipping addresses, view past
orders, track loyalty points, and check the status of a return without
contacting support. An account is not required to place an order; guest
checkout is available for all products.

## Placing an Order

Orders can be placed through the website or the Terra & Pine mobile app.
Once an order is submitted you will receive an order confirmation email
within a few minutes. If you do not receive a confirmation email within one
hour, check your spam folder before contacting support, since most missing
confirmations are caused by email filtering rather than a failed order.

## Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Apple
Pay. We also support financing through a third-party provider for orders
over $150, split into four interest-free installments. Gift cards can be
combined with one other payment method but cannot be used to purchase
additional gift cards.

## Order Changes and Cancellations

Orders can be changed or canceled within 2 hours of purchase by contacting
support, as long as the order has not yet entered fulfillment. After an
order enters fulfillment it cannot be changed, but it can still be returned
once delivered under the standard return policy. See the Cancellation
Policy in our policies document for full details on refund timing.

## Return and Refund Basics

If you need to return an item, contact support within 45 days of delivery to
receive a return authorization number. Returned items must be unused, with
tags attached, and in their original packaging. Footwear must be returned
with the original box, which should be placed inside a shipping box rather
than used as the shipping container itself. Refunds are issued to the
original payment method within 5-7 business days of the warehouse receiving
the return.

## Shipping Basics

Most domestic orders arrive within 3-7 business days depending on the
shipping method selected at checkout. International orders can take longer
due to customs processing. Full details, including cost tiers and
international restrictions, are covered in our shipping document.

## Product Sizing and Fit

Each product page includes a size chart with measurements in both inches and
centimeters. Footwear is true to size for most customers, but we recommend
sizing up half a size if you plan to wear thick hiking socks. If a first
pair doesn't fit, exchanges for a different size are free and do not count
against your standard return window.

## Gift Cards

Digital gift cards are delivered by email within minutes of purchase and
never expire. Physical gift cards can be requested for in-store pickup at
our Bend, Oregon flagship location. Lost digital gift card codes can be
recovered by contacting support with the original order number.

## Loyalty Program

The Trailhead Rewards program earns 1 point per dollar spent, and 100
points can be redeemed for a $5 discount on a future order. Members also
receive early access to seasonal sales and a free birthday gift. Enrollment
is free and happens automatically when you create an account.

## Contacting Customer Support

Support is available Monday through Friday, 8am to 7pm Eastern Time, and
Saturday, 9am to 3pm Eastern Time. Support is not available on Sundays or
on major public holidays. The fastest way to reach us is live chat on the
website; email responses typically take one business day.

## Sustainability Commitment

All insulated jackets use recycled synthetic fill, and our packaging is made
from 100% recycled cardboard with no plastic mailers. We also run a gear
repair program so worn items like broken zippers or torn seams can be fixed
instead of replaced; repair requests can be started from your account order
history.
