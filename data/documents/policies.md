# Company Policies

## Return and Refund Policy

Unworn, unwashed items may be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full
refund to the original payment method. Items marked "final sale" and gift
cards are not eligible for return. Refunds are processed within 5-7
business days after our warehouse receives and inspects the returned item,
and an email confirmation is sent once the refund is issued.

## Exchange Policy

Size and color exchanges are free within the same 45-day window as returns
and do not require a new payment. If the requested size or color is out of
stock, we issue store credit instead so you are not charged twice while
waiting for restock. Exchanges typically ship within 2 business days of the
original item being received at our warehouse.

## Cancellation Policy

Orders can be canceled free of charge within 2 hours of purchase, before
they enter fulfillment. Orders canceled after entering fulfillment cannot be
stopped in transit, but can be returned once delivered under the standard
Return and Refund Policy. Custom-engraved products cannot be canceled once
production has started.

## Warranty Policy

All footwear, backpacks, and hardware components include a 2-year limited
warranty covering manufacturing defects such as sole separation, broken
buckles, and failed zippers. The warranty does not cover damage from normal
wear and tear, accidental damage, improper storage, or repairs performed by
an unauthorized third party. Warranty claims are handled through our gear
repair program and require a copy of the original order confirmation.

## Price Matching Policy

We match a lower price found on an identical, in-stock item from an
authorized retailer within 14 days of purchase. Price matches are not
available against marketplace resellers, clearance sales, or limited-time
flash sales. Approved price matches are refunded as store credit within 3
business days.

## Privacy Policy

We collect the information required to fulfill an order and operate your
account: full name, shipping and billing address, email, phone number, and
payment token (we never store full card numbers ourselves). We also collect
browsing and purchase history to personalize product recommendations and
measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. We do not sell customer
data to third parties. Data may be shared with shipping carriers and
payment processors solely to complete an order. Customers can request a
copy of their stored data, or request deletion of their account and
associated data, by emailing privacy@terrapineoutfitters.example.

## Cookie Policy

Our website uses essential cookies required for checkout and account login,
as well as optional analytics and advertising cookies. Optional cookies can
be declined from the cookie banner shown on your first visit, or changed
later from the "Privacy Preferences" link in the site footer. Declining
optional cookies does not affect your ability to browse or purchase.

## Terms of Sale

All prices are listed in US dollars unless a regional storefront is
selected. We reserve the right to limit quantities on clearance items and to
cancel orders placed at a mispriced amount due to a listing error, in which
case the customer is notified and fully refunded. Sales tax is calculated
based on the shipping destination at checkout.

## Loyalty Program Terms

Trailhead Rewards points are earned on the pre-tax, post-discount order
total and expire after 24 months of account inactivity. Points earned on an
order are reversed if the order is later returned. Points have no cash
value and cannot be transferred between accounts.

## Accessibility Statement

We are committed to making our website usable by people of all abilities
and aim to conform to WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. If you encounter an
accessibility barrier on our website, contact support with the page URL and
a description of the issue so our team can investigate and prioritize a
fix.
