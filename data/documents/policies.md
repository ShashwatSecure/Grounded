# Policies

## Cancellation Policy

Cancellations are allowed within 14 days of purchase for a full refund. After 14
days, cancellations are subject to a 20% restocking fee. Digital products
purchased and downloaded are non-refundable once the download has started.

## Warranty Policy

All hardware products include a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing
defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or
unauthorized repairs.

## Privacy Policy

We only collect the information required to fulfill an order: name, shipping
address, and email. We do not sell customer data to third parties.
