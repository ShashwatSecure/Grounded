# Shipping Information

## Domestic Shipping Options

Standard domestic shipping takes 3-7 business days and costs a flat $6.95.
Orders over $75 ship for free using standard shipping. Expedited shipping
(2-3 business days) is available for $16.95, and overnight shipping is
available for $34.95 on orders placed before 1pm Eastern Time on a business
day. Domestic orders ship from our warehouses in Bend, Oregon and
Columbus, Ohio.

## International Shipping

We currently ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, and all European Union
member countries. International shipping takes 8-18 business days depending
on destination customs processing, and costs are calculated at checkout
based on package weight and destination. International orders may be
subject to import duties and taxes charged by the destination country,
which are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the
checkout price.

## Shipping Restrictions

We are unable to ship camp fuel canisters or bear spray internationally due
to carrier hazardous-materials restrictions; these items are domestic-only.
We cannot deliver to P.O. boxes for orders containing footwear over a
certain size due to package dimensions, though apparel-only orders may use a
P.O. box.

## Order Tracking

A tracking number is emailed and texted (if a phone number was provided) once
an order ships. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the
order leaves the warehouse. If tracking shows no movement for more than 5
business days, contact support so we can open a trace request with the
carrier.

## Delivery Issues and Claims

If a package is marked delivered but not received, wait 48 hours in case of
carrier scanning delays, then contact support to file a claim. Claims for
lost packages must be filed within 30 days of the expected delivery date.
Damaged items should be reported with photos within 7 days of delivery so we
can send a replacement without requiring the damaged item to be returned
first.

## Address Changes

A shipping address can be corrected free of charge as long as the order has
not yet been handed to the carrier. Once a package is in transit, address
changes must be requested directly with the carrier, and Terra & Pine
Outfitters cannot guarantee the carrier will honor the change.

## Holiday Shipping Cutoffs

During the winter holiday season, standard shipping cutoff dates are posted
on the shipping page starting in early November to help ensure delivery
before December 25th. Orders placed after the posted cutoff may still be
eligible for expedited or overnight shipping for an additional fee.

## Carbon-Neutral Shipping Program

All shipments are carbon-offset at no extra cost through a certified
reforestation program. Customers who choose our slowest available shipping
speed also receive 50 bonus Trailhead Rewards points as part of our
low-impact shipping incentive.
