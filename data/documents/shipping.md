# Shipping

## Domestic Shipping

Standard domestic shipping takes 3-5 business days and costs a flat $5.99. Orders
over $50 ship for free. Expedited shipping (1-2 business days) is available for
$14.99.

## International Shipping

International shipping takes 7-21 business days depending on destination
customs processing. International orders may be subject to import duties and
taxes charged by the destination country, which are the responsibility of the
customer.

## Order Tracking

A tracking number is emailed once an order ships. Tracking updates can take up
to 24 hours to appear after the order leaves the warehouse.
